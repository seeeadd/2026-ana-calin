## Recording rules

- Local copy only, never the live site: the live feed puts real registrants' first names on screen.
- `KIT_FORM_ID` empty, survey call stubbed, API blocked, so nothing reaches Kit or her Sheet. The real button can create a real
  sign-up, even on localhost (it falls back to app.kit.com).
- Dates off everywhere: `.tk-k`, `.fmeta`, `.dwhen`, `.xpill`, `#xev`, survey question text.
- Render desktop and phone separately: cursor on desktop, taps at phone width.
