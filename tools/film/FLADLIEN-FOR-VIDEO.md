## What it means for the video (starting points; the source decides)

- **Show, don't tell.** For every line Ana says, ask his question, "how do we show this?", and answer with a real
  thing: her inbox, the form, the calendar, a student's screenshot, her slide. Prefer the page's own pieces
  (`tools/film/SITE-ASSETS.md`).
- **The most important words are the biggest.** Captions and on-screen words: the key phrase large, framing words
  small. Two colours: ink plus the one accent on the words that matter.
- **Motion every few seconds.** Something builds or changes about every 6 s, in the order it is said ("each one pops
  up"), not all at once.
- **One idea per shot.** Split rather than crowd; shots are free.
- **Lists read top to bottom,** never across.
- **The reveal:** cover most of the frame, then put the new line on top.
- **The framework comes back with progress:** the Day 1 to 3 map returns each section with where we are.
- **Tease what's coming,** cut off at the edge, early in the video.
- **Proof as raw screenshots,** labelled "from Ana's past challenge, results vary".
