## What they teach

1. **Keep one anchor and change the world around it.** The horse, the woman on the train, the suitcase, the elevator
   door. The eye rests on the constant and reads the change as progress.
2. **Transitions are objects in the story**: a door, an elevator, a curtain, a canvas that becomes the sea, a red
   thread, a page turning, an iris. Never a generic fade or slide.
3. **Real animation, never stop motion** (Sean: "want real animation, not just stop motion"). Motion is
   continuous, at full frame rate, and follows the animator's principles:
   - anticipation: a small move the other way first;
   - easing: slow in, slow out;
   - arcs, not straight lines;
   - follow-through and settle: overshoot a little, then come to rest;
   - overlapping action: parts arrive a beat apart;
   - secondary motion: leaves sway after the door moves.

   The handmade pieces (m03, m07, m13) are a reference for texture only, not for their stepped timing.
4. **Words arrive as they're said**, one at a time, written by hand or set small in a serif, with a big image and
   small type (Hedy, Sharpie).
5. **The camera discovers.** A pan along a drawing rewards looking (Bergdorf, Atelier Choux).
6. **Idle loops keep a still alive without asking for attention**: a foot bobbing, a cat breathing, steam, a curtain
   in the breeze.
7. **Every piece is a small story**: a state, a turn, a payoff, and the brand or date last.
8. **Marketers sell the life, not the feature.** Rimowa sells the journey, "Becoming Her" the identity, Atelier Choux
   the anticipation. The product appears as a companion inside that life.
9. **Reveals are theatre**: curtain, door, iris. The thing waited for comes last.
10. **Restraint.** One palette, one texture family, a few moving things at once. The calm ones feel the most
    expensive.

## What a marketer wants this page to make people feel

The visitor already chose the free seat. VIP must feel like keeping it and having Ana closer, never like being
chased. The feelings, in order:
- **relief**: I won't miss anything, and it stays mine;
- **company**: I won't be stuck alone with my draft;
- **clarity**: my mess becomes one offer;
- **momentum**: by Friday something real goes out.

Motion must say continuity, closeness and progress. It must never say urgency: no pulsing, no countdowns, no shaking
buttons.
