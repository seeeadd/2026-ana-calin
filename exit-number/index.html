How this is calculated

Purchases needed. Your monthly goal divided by the price, rounded up to a whole purchase.

With costs. Each purchase leaves the price, minus the percentage selling costs and allowance, minus the cash cost per purchase. Profit is that amount times the purchases, minus monthly fixed costs. For a profit goal, purchases needed are the goal plus fixed costs, divided by what each purchase leaves, rounded up.

Audience. Purchases needed divided by the number of distinct people who receive the offer, assuming each person buys once. Your own rate works the other way: purchases needed divided by the rate, rounded up.

Time. Shared monthly hours, plus purchases times hours per purchaser, plus groups times hours per group when you teach in groups. Hours available a month are hours a week times 52, divided by 12.

What it doesn’t do. It doesn’t predict how many people will buy, convert currencies, or include personal tax. Numbers are kept exactly and rounded only when shown.
